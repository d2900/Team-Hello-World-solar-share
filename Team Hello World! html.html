About SolarShare

Don't have a rooftop for solar? You can still go solar.

Many homes and apartments can't fit rooftop solar panels. SolarShare lets you subscribe to a share of a Tata Power community solar project instead, and see the benefit as a credit on your own electricity bill.

  1. The rooftop problem

    Not every home has a roof that's suitable for solar — shade, structure, rented flats or apartment living all get in the way of installing your own panels.

  2. The SolarShare concept

    SolarShare lets you subscribe to a share of a large, professionally-built Tata Power solar project, without buying or installing any equipment.

  3. Your solar participation

    You choose a plan sized to your usage. Your share of the project's output is set aside for you as it's generated.

  4. Bill credits & savings

    The value of your solar share is calculated and applied as a credit on your existing Tata Power electricity bill, lowering what you pay, month after month.

  5. Track your impact

    Your dashboard shows your running savings, solar credits and the clean energy generated on your behalf, so you can see your impact grow over time.

How it works

A shared solar project lets you participate without installing panels on your own roof.

  1. Solar farm

    A shared solar project generates clean electricity.

  2. Your solar share

    You subscribe to a share of the project — no panels on your own roof.

  3. Bill credit

    Your eligible solar benefit is reflected as a credit on your electricity bill.

No rooftop No installation No maintenance

You're subscribing to a share of a Tata Power community solar project — not physical equipment. As the project generates electricity, your eligible share is estimated and reflected as a credit on your existing Tata Power bill. You don't receive electricity directly from a specific panel.
