The Idea

What if living in an apartment didn't mean missing out on solar?

SolarShare is a UI/UX concept that makes community solar easier to understand and access for urban renters who may not have a rooftop.

The experience connects community solar directly to something users already understand — their electricity bill.

⭐ The Big Idea — Bill-to-Solar Simulator

Instead of asking users to understand technical solar units first, we start with their monthly bill.

₹2,000 monthly bill → Estimated ₹600 solar credit → ₹1,400 estimated bill

Users can enter their own bill amount and see an illustrative estimate of the potential solar-credit impact.

This turns an unfamiliar energy concept into a simple, personal decision.

Why SolarShare?

No rooftop shouldn't mean no access to solar.

We designed SolarShare to make a complex energy concept feel as familiar as checking your electricity bill.

The goal is simple: make solar easier to understand, easier to trust, and easier to participate in.

Prototype Note

This is a UI/UX concept prototype created for the design challenge. It is not a live Tata Power service.

All plans, savings, credits, generation values, projects, payments, and other numerical information shown are illustrative and are not connected to a real Tata Power account, billing system, payment system, or solar project.
