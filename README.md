Solar savings, without a rooftop.

What if living in an apartment didn't mean missing out on solar?

SolarShare is a UI/UX concept that makes community solar simple for urban renters — from understanding the idea to seeing its potential impact on their electricity bill.

The Big Idea — Bill-to-Solar Simulator

Instead of asking users to understand solar units, plans, or technical terms, we start with something they already know:

Their electricity bill.

₹2,000 monthly bill → Estimated ₹600 solar credit → ₹1,400 estimated bill

This turns an unfamiliar solar concept into a simple, personal financial decision.

The Experience

Understand → Simulate → Choose → Subscribe → Track

☀️ Visual onboarding — Understand community solar simply.

💰 Savings Simulator — See potential bill impact before choosing a plan.

📋 Simple plan comparison — Choose based on participation and estimated impact.

📊 SolarShare Dashboard — Track credits, bill impact, and solar generation.

🌱 Project transparency — See the solar project behind your subscription.

🌐 English + Hindi — Designed for accessibility and wider adoption.

Why SolarShare?

No rooftop shouldn't mean no access to solar.

We designed the experience to make a complex energy concept feel as familiar as checking your electricity bill.
